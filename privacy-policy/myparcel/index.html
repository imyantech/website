Privacy Policy for MyParcel

Effective Date: September 2, 2026

Welcome to MyParcel, developed by iMyanTech. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

When you use MyParcel, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, and phone number when you register for an account.
  • Logistics Data: Information related to parcels, such as sender/recipient names, addresses, phone numbers, and delivery status.
  • Device Information: We may request access to your device's Camera (for scanning QR codes and taking parcel photos) and Storage.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To provide, operate, and maintain our App's core logistics and delivery tracking features.
  • To manage your account, verify your identity, and provide customer support.
  • To send you important system updates and push notifications regarding your parcels and account status.

3. Sharing of Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your data is stored securely and is only shared with authorized business partners or riders directly involved in the delivery process of your specific parcels within the MyParcel system.

4. Data Security

We use Firebase (Google) and MongoDB to store your data securely. We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

5. App Permissions

The App may request the following permissions:

  • Camera: Required to scan QR/Barcodes and take photos of parcels.
  • Notifications: Required to send you real-time updates regarding parcel deliveries and system announcements.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top.
